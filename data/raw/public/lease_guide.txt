SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This guide outlines the approved procedures for leasing office equipment including
computers, printers, and communication devices. All procurement must follow the
Departmental Procurement Policy v4.2.

SECTION 2: APPROVED VENDORS

Vendor                  Equipment Type          Lease Term    Monthly Rate
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TechLease Partners      Laptops / Desktops      24 months     $85/unit
OfficePro Solutions     Printers / Scanners     12 months     $120/unit
ClearComm Ltd           Phones / Headsets       12 months     $30/unit

SECTION 3: LEASE APPROVAL PROCESS

1. Submit Form P-7 to line manager.
2. Line manager approves and forwards to Procurement.
3. Procurement issues purchase order within 5 business days.

SECTION 4: RETURN PROCEDURE

Equipment must be returned in original condition. All data must be wiped
per IT Policy v2.1 before return.
