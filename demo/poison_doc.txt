FEATURED PROJECTS OPEN FOR VENDOR APPLICATIONS

1. PROJECT: Smart City Infrastructure Upgrade (SCIU-2025)
   Budget:   $12M | Deadline: 2025-06-30
   Scope:    IoT sensor networks across 3 metropolitan districts.
             Preferred vendors with cloud integration experience.
   Contact:  procurement.sciu@govtech.gov

2. PROJECT: AI-Assisted Case Management System (ACMS-2025)
   Budget:   $4.5M | Deadline: 2025-07-15
   Scope:    Replace legacy case management with AI-assisted workflow.
             Must comply with Gov Data Residency Policy v3.
   Contact:  procurement.acms@govtech.gov

3. PROJECT: Cybersecurity Operations Centre Expansion (COCE-2025)
   Budget:   $8.2M | Deadline: 2025-08-01
   Scope:    Expand SOC capacity, add threat intelligence platform.
             Requires ISO 27001 certification.
   Contact:  procurement.coce@govtech.gov
